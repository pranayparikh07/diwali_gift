What actually happened?
You opened a webpage. It displayed a warning to demonstrate why an unexpected QR code should be checked before it is trusted.
Fake sign-in pagesCopied login pages can steal credentials that people type into them.
Payment scamsFraudulent QR links can lead to fake payment pages or requests.
Unsafe downloadsSome links try to trick people into installing harmful apps or files.
Look-alike websitesFraudsters use familiar branding and urgency to win trust.
  • Preview a QR code's destination before opening it.
  • Check the domain carefully before signing in or paying.
  • Never enter a password, OTP or payment details to claim an unexpected gift.
  • Verify offers through an official channel.
